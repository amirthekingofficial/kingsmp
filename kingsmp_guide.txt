1. 👑 THE MONARCHY, ELECTIONS & CROWN TIER BUFFS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of KingSMP is to claim, upgrade, and protect the Royal Crown.
Up to 5 players can hold the rank of King simultaneously.

- Claiming the Crown:
  * Elections: Started automatically every 250 Minecraft days (or manually by an
    Admin). Players vote for candidates using "/vote <player>". The winners are
    crowned and receive a Level 1 Crown.
  * Usurping: If you possess a Royal Crown (e.g. by looting it or picking it up
    from a fallen King) and there is an open King slot, you instantly claim the
    reign and become King.

- Crown Degradation & Death:
  * If a King dies, they are dethroned and the Crown's level degrades by 1 (unless 
    it was already at Level 5/MAX).
  * If a King leaves the server while in PvP combat, they are instantly dethroned.

- Ender Chest Restriction:
  * The Royal Crown and the Dragon Egg are sacred items of power and cannot be
    stored in Ender Chests (regular or expanded). If placed inside, they will
    violently escape back into the player's inventory or drop to the ground.

- Placing Restriction:
  * The Royal Crown (which is a carved pumpkin under the hood) cannot be placed
    as a block.

- Crown Upgrades & Level Buffs:
  To upgrade, hold the Crown in your inventory and run the "/upgrade" command
  while holding the necessary materials.

  * 👑 Level 1 (The King)
    - Potion Effects: Speed I, Strength I, Resistance I, Hero of the Village I
    - Attribute Modifiers: +2.0 Armor
    - Max Health Modifier: +4 Hearts (+8.0 max health)
    - Materials Required to Unlock: Initial election or usurpation.

  * 👑 Level 2 (The Sovereign)
    - Potion Effects: Speed II, Strength I, Resistance I, Fire Resistance, Hero of the Village I
    - Attribute Modifiers: +3.0 Armor, +2.0 Armor Toughness
    - Max Health Modifier: +6 Hearts (+12.0 max health)
    - Upgrade Cost: 32x Diamond, 16x Gold Block, 1x Ominous Bottle

  * 👑 Level 3 (The Netherite Monarch)
    - Potion Effects: Speed II, Strength II, Resistance I, Fire Resistance, Hero of the Village I
    - Attribute Modifiers: +5.0 Armor, +3.0 Armor Toughness, +0.1 Knockback Resistance
    - Max Health Modifier: +10 Hearts (+20.0 max health)
    - Upgrade Cost: 4x Netherite Ingot, 64x Diamond, 1x Nether Star

  * 👑 Level 4 (The Void Lord)
    - Potion Effects: Speed II, Strength II, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, Regeneration I, Hero of the Village I
    - Attribute Modifiers: +7.0 Armor, +4.0 Armor Toughness, +0.2 Knockback Resistance
    - Max Health Modifier: +14 Hearts (+28.0 max health)
    - Upgrade Cost: 2x Netherite Block, 3x Nether Star, 8x Echo Shard

  * 👑 Level 5 (The Emperor - MAX TIER)
    - Potion Effects: Speed III, Strength III, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, Regeneration II, Hero of the Village I
    - Attribute Modifiers: +10.0 Armor, +6.0 Armor Toughness, +0.3 Knockback Resistance
    - Max Health Modifier: +20 Hearts (+40.0 max health)
    - Upgrade Cost: 1x Heavy Core, 1x Dragon Egg, 4x Netherite Block, 5x Nether Star

2. 🛡️ FACTION RANKS, BUFFS & TERRITORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kings can establish factions, recruit players, promote officers, and manage land.

- Creating Factions:
  * Costs 5,000 Silver. Run "/faction create <name>". The founder becomes Faction King.

- Faction Ranks & Buffs:
  Members receive permanent status effects based on their faction rank:
  * 👑 King: Permanent Haste I, Fire Resistance, and Hero of the Village I.
  * ⚡ Commander: Permanent Speed I, Haste I, Fire Resistance, and Hero of the Village I.
  * 🛡️ Knight: Permanent Resistance I.
  * ⚓ Recruit: Base rank, receives no permanent buffs.

- Faction Outposts:
  * Kings and Commanders can mark outposts with "/faction setoutpost <name>".
  * The first outpost is FREE. Subsequent outposts cost 1,000 Silver.
  * Outposts can be deleted using "/faction deleteoutpost <name>".
  * Members can teleport to outposts with "/faction outpost <name>" (blocked in combat).

- Faction Tax System:
  * Faction Kings can set a tax rate (1% to 5%) using "/faction tax <rate>".
  * Faction taxes apply when faction members spend Silver in the economy.
  * Important: Taxes are generated by the system, meaning they cost the spending
    player NO extra money.
  * Taxed Silver goes directly into the King's personal balance.
  * Cap: A faction can generate a maximum of 5,000 Silver in tax revenue for their
    King per calendar day.

3. ⚔️ PVP COMBAT RESTRAINTS & LOGGING PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To maintain competitive integrity, hitting another player or taking damage from another
player triggers a Combat Tag.

- The Tagged State:
  * Duration: 15 seconds.
  * Display: Shows red bold text above your hotbar: "⚔️ IN COMBAT: Xs remaining ⚔️".

- Restrictions in Combat:
  While combat tagged, players CANNOT:
  * Teleport home (" /home", "/home tp")
  * Teleport to shared homes (" /home tp <player>")
  * Warp to faction outposts (" /faction outpost <name>")
  * Request or accept teleport requests ("/tpa", "/tpaccept")
  * Use economy commands ("/shop", "/market", "/sell", "/auction", "/bounty")
  * Teleport randomly ("/rtp")

- Combat Logging Penalty:
  If you disconnect from the server while in combat:
  * You drop your entire inventory (including armor and offhand slots) on the ground.
  * If you are a King, you are instantly dethroned, and your Crown is dropped.

4. 🎒 ENDER CHEST UPGRADES & REMOTE ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Keep your items safe and access them anywhere:

- Remote Access:
  * Run "/enderchest" or "/ec" to open your Ender Chest from anywhere.
  * Cost to Unlock: A one-time payment of 32x Diamonds.

- Size Expansion:
  * Run "/ec upgrade" to expand your remote and physical Ender Chest to double chest
    capacity (6 rows, 54 slots).
  * Cost to Upgrade: A one-time payment of 2x Netherite Ingots.
  * When upgraded, any existing items in the normal chest are migrated safely.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. 🗺️ RANDOM TELEPORTATION (RTP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Teleport randomly to find resources or bases safely:

- Command: "/rtp" (blocked while in combat).
- Safety Mechanics:
  * Teleports you to safe, solid coordinates within the world border.
  * Excludes hazardous blocks such as lava, magma blocks, cacti, fire, and liquids.
  * Safe locations are search-cached asynchronously (up to 10) to prevent server lag.

6. 📜 DAILY CONTRACTS (QUESTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Earn Silver and Diamonds by executing daily tasks:

- Commands:
  * Open Quest Menu: "/quest"
  * Accept: "/quest accept <easy|medium|hard>" (Only 1 quest active at a time)
  * Progress: "/quest status"
  * Cancel/Abandon: "/quest cancel" or "/quest abandon"

- Quest Payouts:
  * Easy: 200 Silver (No item rewards)
  * Medium: 500 Silver + 1x Diamond
  * Hard: 1,000 Silver + 4x Diamonds

- Quest Pools (Rotate daily based on calendar day):
  * Easy Quests (Mine 32 Iron Ores, Mine 64 Coal Ores, Mine 32 Copper Ores, Mine 
    16 Gold Ores, Mine 64 Redstone Ores, Hunt 12 Skeletons, Hunt 10 Spiders, 
    Hunt 15 Creepers, Hunt 20 Zombies)
  * Medium Quests (Hunt 10 Zombies, Hunt 8 Endermen, Hunt 15 Blazes, Hunt 12 
    Piglins, Mine 45 Gold Ores, Mine 24 Lapis Ores, Mine 16 Nether Quartz Ores)
  * Hard Quests (Mine 128 Diamond Ores, Mine 20 Ancient Debris, Mine 15 Emerald 
    Ores, Hunt 4 Wither Skeletons, Hunt 6 Ghasts, Hunt 25 Drowned)

10. 📝 PLAYER & ECONOMY COMMAND REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Below is the complete registry of commands available on the server.

* Crown & Election:
  - "/vote <player>": Vote for a player during an active election.
  - "/myvote": View your current vote.
  - "/status": View the current Kings, reign durations, and active election phase.
  - "/leaderboard": Check the longest-reigning Kings in server history.
  - "/upgrade": Upgrades the Royal Crown in your inventory if you have materials.

* Faction Management:
  - "/faction create <name>": Create a new Faction (costs 5,000 Silver).
  - "/faction invite <player>": Invite a player to your faction.
  - "/faction accept": Accept a pending invitation.
  - "/faction promote <player> <rank>": Promotes a member (COMMANDER, KNIGHT, RECRUIT) (King only).
  - "/faction kick <player>": Kicks a player from the Faction (King/Commanders only).
  - "/faction view": Open faction GUI showing members and status.
  - "/faction leave": Leave your current faction.
  - "/faction disband": Disband the Faction (King only).
  - "/faction setoutpost <name>": Mark faction outpost at your location (First free, others cost 1,000 Silver).
  - "/faction outpost <name>": Teleport to a faction outpost (blocked in combat).
  - "/faction deleteoutpost <name>": Delete a faction outpost.
  - "/faction listoutposts": List all outposts in your faction.
  - "/faction tax [rate]": Check or set faction tax rate (1% to 5%) (King only).

* Economy & Commerce:
  - "/shop": Opens the Global Market GUI.
  - "/shop sell <price>": List the item in your hand on the player market.
  - "/market cancel <id>": Cancel player market listing and return item.
  - "/sell": Opens the Bank Sell GUI to sell items.
  - "/price": Appraise the item in your main hand.
  - "/pay <player> <amount>": Transfer Silver from your balance to another player.
  - "/bal" or "/balance": View your current virtual Silver balance.
  - "/bounty add <player> <amount>": Place a bounty on a target player (minimum 50 Silver).
  - "/bounty list": List all active player bounties.

* Teleportation & Utility:
  - "/rtp": Randomly teleport within safe bounds (blocked in combat).
  - "/home set": Set your home point.
  - "/home tp" or "/home": Teleport to your home point (blocked in combat).
  - "/home share <player>": Toggles sharing your home access with another player.
  - "/home tp <player>": Teleport to a shared home (blocked in combat).
  - "/home delete": Delete your current home point.
  - "/tpa <player>": Request to teleport to a player (60s cooldown per player, 10s general spam protection).
  - "/tpaccept [player]": Accept teleport request (blocked in combat).
  - "/tpadeny [player]": Deny teleport request (blocked in combat).
  - "/tpauto": Toggle auto-accepting teleport requests (blocked in combat).
  - "/enderchest" or "/ec": Open remote Ender Chest (Costs 32 Diamonds to unlock).
  - "/ec upgrade" or "/enderchest upgrade": Expand Ender Chest to 54 slots (Costs 2 Netherite Ingots).
  - "/quest": Open Daily Contracts GUI.
  - "/quest accept <easy|medium|hard>": Accept a daily quest.
  - "/quest status": View your active quest details.
  - "/quest cancel" or "/quest abandon": Abandon active quest.

11. ⚙️ ADMIN & OPERATOR COMMAND REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All admin commands are subcommands of "/king" and require OP status:

- "/king forceupgrade <player> <level>": Instantly set player's crown to level 1-5.
- "/king election start [seconds]": Start an election (default is 10 minutes/12000 ticks).
- "/king election end": End an active election immediately.
- "/king reset": Wipe all active kings, votes, and reset election states.
- "/king crown <player>": Instantly crown a player (if open slots).
- "/king dethrone <player>": Instantly remove a player's crown.
- "/king mint <player> <amount>": Grant Silver to a player.
- "/king remove <player> <amount>": Deduct Silver from a player's balance.
- "/king set <player> <amount>": Force-set a player's Silver balance.
- "/king bounty set <player> <amount>": Force-set a player's bounty amount.
- "/king bounty remove <player>": Clear player's bounty.
- "/king unlockender <player> [large]": Instantly unlock regular or large remote Ender Chest for player.
- "/king quest view <player>": Check active quest details for player.
- "/king quest reset <player>": Clear player's active quest.
- "/king quest complete <player>": Force-complete player's active quest and award rewards.
- "/king market remove <id>": Remove a listing from player market by ID.
- "/king reign set <player> <ticks>": Set total reign ticks for player.
- "/king reign clear <player>": Clear total reign ticks for player.
- "/king combat clear <player>": Instantly clear a player's combat tag.
